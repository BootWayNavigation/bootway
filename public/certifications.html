Trust & compliance

Certifications: ISO 27001, DPIIT, PwD validated

Independent recognition for information security, Startup India status, and accessibility — the credentials hospitals and campuses ask for before they deploy indoor navigation.

ISO 27001:2022 — Information security

BootWay iNaaS Private Limited is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for information security management. Certificate reference: INQ/AN-23454/1/0926. This covers how we protect systems and data used to deliver indoor navigation and virtual tours.

View ISO 27001 certificate (PDF)

Startup India — DPIIT recognised

BootWay is recognised by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under Startup India. Recognition number: DIPP189212. This confirms BootWay’s status as an Indian startup building indoor navigation from Jaipur.

View DPIIT certificate (PDF)

PwD accessibility validation

Accessible routing — including step-free and lift-aware paths — has been validated for persons with disabilities. Certificate of training and accessibility validation from Adarsh Gyanodaya Vikas Samiti (reference 481/AGVS/SWM). Hospitals can offer wheelchair-friendly indoor directions without a separate app.

View PwD certificate (PDF)
