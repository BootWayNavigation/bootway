Boilerplate

BootWay (BootWay iNaaS Private Limited) is a Jaipur-based indoor navigation company. Visitors scan a QR code and get turn-by-turn directions inside hospitals, campuses, and large buildings in their own language, with no app and no beacons. BootWay also builds 360° virtual tours. Delivered for CK Birla Hospitals, S.M.S. Medical College, Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, and Rajasthan University of Health Sciences. Certifications: ISO 27001:2022, Startup India DPIIT recognised, PwD accessibility validated.
