Why campuses need digital wayfinding

Spread-out institutes are hard to walk on the first visit. Parents attending counselling, freshers looking for a lab, and guests arriving for conferences stop at every security desk. Static maps go out of date when a department moves. A campus navigation system should work on any phone browser, support local languages, and update when the layout changes — without forcing an app download.

BootWay is a Jaipur-based indoor navigation company. The same QR-based approach used for hospital wayfinding maps university and medical-college campuses across blocks and floors.

How BootWay campus navigation works

  1. Scan a QR code at the gate, reception, or a printed orientation handout. The map opens in the browser.
  2. Choose the destination — lecture hall, lab, admin office, hostel, or auditorium.
  3. Follow turn-by-turn directions across outdoor paths and indoor floors, with lift or stairs options where needed.

Open days, parent visits, and remote orientation

Pair campus maps with a 360° virtual tour so families can walk key blocks before they arrive. On the day of the visit, QR codes at the gate continue the journey on site. Institutes use both for counselling weeks, convocations, and conferences.

Medical colleges sit between campus and hospital workflows. Students and attendants need paths to lecture halls and OPDs. BootWay’s hospital and campus products share the same QR-in-browser model, so an institute can start with academic blocks and extend into clinical areas without forcing a second app on visitors.

Administrators typically start with the highest-footfall destinations: main gate, admin, libraries, exam halls, and hostels. Languages should match the students and parents who actually visit — often Hindi and English on Rajasthan campuses — with accessible routes where lifts and ramps exist.

What a campus project typically includes

  • Digital mapping of blocks, floors, and high-traffic destinations
  • QR access points at gates and orientation desks
  • Indoor and between-building routes
  • Accessible / lift-aware options
  • Staff briefing and layout update support

FAQ

What is a campus navigation system?

It guides students, parents, and visitors to lecture halls, labs, hostels, and admin offices with turn-by-turn directions — instead of relying only on static campus maps.

Does BootWay need an app or beacons?

No. Visitors scan a QR code and get directions in the browser. No mobile app and no Bluetooth beacons.
