Collecting and using your personal data

Types of data collected

Personal data

We may ask you to provide personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Organisation name and facility details you share in enquiry forms
  • Usage Data

When you use QR-based indoor navigation in a building we have mapped, the route experience generally runs in the browser without requiring you to create a visitor account. We may still collect technical Usage Data needed to operate and improve navigation reliability.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. It may include your device’s IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on pages, unique device identifiers, and diagnostic data. When you access the Service through a mobile device, we may also collect device type, OS, browser type, and similar diagnostics.

Information from third-party services

Where account login is offered (for example candidate or staff portals), you may be able to sign in with a third-party identity provider such as Google. If you grant access, we may receive your name, email address, and profile information associated with that account, and will use it only to operate the relevant account features.

Tracking technologies and cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Website, understand traffic, and improve the Service. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies; some features may not work fully if cookies are disabled. We may use session cookies and persistent cookies, including:

  • Necessary / essential cookies — required for basic site function and security.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices such as language or notice acceptance.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how pages are used (for example Google Analytics, where enabled).

Advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads where ads are enabled on the Website, subject to your browser settings and applicable consent requirements.

Use of your personal data

We may use Personal Data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Respond to enquiries, demos, and support requests
  • Manage accounts and applications (including careers)
  • Perform contracts for products or services you purchase or commission
  • Send service-related notices and, where permitted, relevant product updates (you may opt out of non-essential marketing)
  • Analyse usage trends and improve security
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Sharing of your personal data

We may share information with Service Providers who help us host, analyse, or deliver the Service; with affiliates under this Privacy Policy; with business partners when you ask us to collaborate; in connection with a merger or asset sale (with notice where required); with public authorities when legally required; or with your consent.

Retention

We retain Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes in this Policy, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Usage Data is generally retained for a shorter period unless needed for security or legal reasons.

Transfer

Your information may be processed at our offices in India and by Service Providers in other jurisdictions. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards for any transfer.

Your rights and deletion

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of Personal Data we hold about you. Contact us at support@bootway.in. We may retain information when we have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so.

Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect Personal Data. BootWay maintains an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified information security management system for how we protect systems and data used to deliver our services. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children’s privacy

Our Service is not directed to anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Links to other websites

Our Service may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of every site you visit.
