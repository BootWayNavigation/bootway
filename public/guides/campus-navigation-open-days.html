Guide · Campuses

Campus navigation for open days and new students

Reduce “where is the auditorium?” chaos during admissions, counselling, and the first weeks of term.

Universities and medical colleges in India concentrate strangers on campus on the same days: entrance counselling, open days, convocations, and hostel allotment. Signboards and student volunteers help, but peak hours still create clusters of lost families around admin blocks and exam halls. This guide focuses on visitor journeys — parents, applicants, and new students — not staff logistics.

Where campus wayfinding breaks

Campuses are often larger than a single hospital wing and more fragmented. Academic blocks, hostels, sports grounds, and admin offices may sit across roads that feel obvious to faculty and confusing to visitors. Temporary rooms for counselling change every season. Printed maps fold poorly, go out of date, and rarely match the language mix of arriving families.

Open days amplify the problem. Multiple parallel sessions mean people must move between halls on a tight clock. A ten-minute wrong turn can mean missing a counselling slot. Volunteers burn out answering the same corridor question hundreds of times.

Design journeys, not just maps

List the journeys that matter for your next peak event:

  • Main gate → registration / document verification
  • Registration → counselling hall or exam room
  • Hall → fee counter or bank desk
  • Gate → hostel allotment / warden office
  • Parking or drop-off → auditorium for orientation

For each journey, note language needs, accessibility, and whether the destination name on the appointment SMS matches the sign on the door. Inconsistent naming is one of the most common hidden causes of lost visitors.

What works on open day

  1. QR at the gate and at bus drop zones — open a browser route without installing an app.
  2. Destinations named the way SMS and emails name them — not only official building codes.
  3. Volunteer scripts that point to the QR first — humans remain for exceptions; digital handles volume.
  4. Accessible options for parents with mobility needs — see accessible indoor navigation.
  5. A short virtual preview before arrival — optional 360° tour of key blocks so families recognise façades.

After admissions week

The same system should serve quieter days: new faculty, patients visiting a medical college hospital attached to campus, conference guests, and alumni events. If navigation only exists as a PDF for one weekend, you will rebuild the work every year. Treat campus navigation as ongoing infrastructure — the same mindset as BootWay campus navigation.

Checklist for facilities and student affairs

  • Agree a single destination glossary with academic and admin teams.
  • Place QR codes where people already stop (gates, help desks), not only on a website.
  • Test routes on mid-range Android phones with average connectivity.
  • Update maps when temporary counselling rooms move.
  • Measure desk questions before and after deployment for one peak event.
