# BootWay

> BootWay is a Jaipur-based indoor navigation company. Visitors scan a QR code and follow turn-by-turn directions inside hospitals, campuses and large buildings, in their own language, with no app and no beacons. BootWay also builds 360° virtual tours.

## Key facts
- Company: BootWay iNaaS Private Limited, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India
- Delivered for: CK Birla Hospitals, S.M.S. Medical College, Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, Rajasthan University of Health Sciences
- 18 lakh+ sq ft of indoor navigation built (Dainik Bhaskar, 1 Oct 2026)
- Certifications: ISO 27001:2022, Startup India DPIIT recognised, PwD accessibility validated
- Contact: support@bootway.in, +91 7976866822
- Office: 3rd Floor, LUB Skill Hub, Sitapura Industrial Area, Jaipur

## One-liner
BootWay gives hospitals turn-by-turn indoor directions that open from a QR code: no app, no beacons, in the visitor's language.
