About BootWay

We make large buildings easy to walk through

BootWay is a Jaipur-based indoor navigation company. Visitors scan a QR code and get turn-by-turn directions inside hospitals, campuses, and large venues — in their own language, with no app and no beacons.

Our story

BootWay iNaaS Private Limited was founded in June 2023 by Samarth Gupta and Arnav Sharma. The idea started from a simple observation: Indian hospitals and campuses keep growing floors and departments, but visitors still rely on asking strangers for directions. Static signage helps, yet it cannot keep up when OPDs move, languages differ, or a wheelchair user needs a step-free path.

We built iNaaS — indoor Navigation as a Service — so facilities can publish accurate routes that open in the browser. No download friction. No Bluetooth beacon hardware to maintain. Just a digital model of the building and QR codes at the points people already stop: gates, reception desks, and prescription slips.

What we build

  • Hospital indoor navigation — gate to OPD, lab, ward, or desk in local languages
  • Campus navigation — open days, hostels, admin blocks, and lecture halls
  • 360° virtual tours — searchable tours with hotspots and floor mini-maps

Across deployments we have mapped 18 lakh+ sq ft of indoor space. Work includes facilities such as CK Birla Hospitals, S.M.S. Medical College (LOI for Dhanvantri OPD), Mahatma Gandhi Hospital Jaipur, and Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS).

How a BootWay route works

  1. Scan the QR code — at an entrance, desk, or printed slip. The map opens in the browser.
  2. Choose the destination — search by department, doctor, room, or landmark.
  3. Follow the path — turn-by-turn guidance that adapts across floors, with lift or stairs options and distance/time remaining.

Directions can run in Hindi, English, and other local languages. Accessible routing supports step-free and lift-aware paths where the building allows it.

Trust and compliance

Hospitals and campuses ask who stands behind the software. BootWay is ISO 27001:2022 certified for information security, recognised under Startup India (DPIIT), and PwD accessibility validated for inclusive routing practices.
