About BootWay

We make large buildings easy to walk through

BootWay is an Indoor Navigation as a Service provider. We build a digital model of your facility, then hand visitors simple directions — so nobody has to stop and ask where to go.

  1. Scan the QR code

    Placed at entrances, reception desks, or printed on a prescription. The map opens straight in the browser.

  2. Choose the destination

    Search by department, doctor, or room number, and set the starting point from a nearby landmark.

  3. Follow the path

    Turn-by-turn guidance to the door, with the route adapting at every change of floor.
